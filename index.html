Intel just had its worst day since the 1970s.

Nasdaq enters correction as AI stocks sell off

The Nasdaq fell into correction territory Friday after weak Amazon and Google earnings and a 29% plunge in Intel, per Semafor. Yahoo Finance put the AI-linked rout at about $5 trillion, and S&P Global moved Meta's outlook to negative, citing its heavy capital spending. Stock futures rose Sunday night, and the FT said the global sell-off was easing.

How each side saw it

Abroad The Financial Times said the global AI sell-off was easing after the Nasdaq's worst month in more than a year, and quoted a portfolio manager who sees no slowdown in the data but says rethinking AI means rethinking growth.

No strikes before Tuesday. After that, 'anything's possible.'

Qatar hosts third day of US-Iran war talks

Iranian negotiators met Qatari mediators in Doha on Sunday for the third straight day, aiming to end the eight-month US-Israeli war on Iran, Reuters reported. Foreign Minister Abbas Araqchi said a plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within two days of a deal is under discussion. Trump has ruled out new attacks before the November 3 midterms but not afterward.

How each side saw it

Left NPR described a Gulf 'on edge,' Bloomberg reported Gulf states fear harsher Iranian retaliation if the US strikes again, and AP quoted the UAE saying military solutions offer no quick way out.

Right RealClearWorld framed the story as the Persian Gulf scrambling to keep the war from restarting.

Abroad The Times of Israel led with the Iranian army's warning that a new war could outlast the last one, while the FT and Middle East Eye focused on Gulf states, which say they are 'braced for the worst.'

Projectiles or mines? Either way, tankers are exploding in the Gulf.

Tanker struck near Saudi coast; Qatari LNG carrier attacked

UKMTO said a tanker was hit by two unidentified projectiles 72 nautical miles east of Saudi Arabia's Ras al-Khafji, with all crew accounted for. The UAE condemned an attack on Qatar's LNG carrier Al Mayeda. Iran's IRGC Navy said two tankers exploded after hitting mines in Hormuz, and the reports do not make clear whether the incidents are linked.

How each side saw it

Abroad Reuters carried the IRGC Navy's account that the blasts came from mines, and Middle East Eye led with UKMTO's report of projectiles and the UAE's condemnation of the Al Mayeda attack.

US pushes to restore Russian gas to Europe via Ukraine

The Wall Street Journal reported on November 2 that the US is pushing to resume Russian gas sales to Europe through Ukraine. The aim is to unlock an energy ceasefire between Moscow and Kyiv. The WSJ said European capitals are resisting.

AmericasMagnitude 3Story 5 of 5

From 50% tariffs to 'the concept of a very good deal.'

Trump and Carney signal progress on trade deal

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney called his conversation with Trump constructive and said negotiators will work toward a deal 'in the coming weeks.' Trump said the sides have 'the concept of a very good deal' and mentioned the Keystone XL pipeline. After talks collapsed in August, Trump imposed 50% tariffs on about $20 billion of Canadian goods.

How each side saw it

Left PBS NewsHour, carrying AP, focused on the timing ahead of the midterms and quoted historian Robert Bothwell calling the thaw 'five minutes of variable sunshine. Rain later.'

Right The Dispatch ran a legal critique, reporting on a lawsuit that calls the administration's tariff war on Canada illegal.

Abroad The Hindu and Reuters stayed close to what the two leaders said, while Politico Europe covered the EU-Canada summit and a six-month timeline to define 'associate membership.'

5 outlets: 1 left, 1 right, 3 abroad
The aftershock in markets

A deal could ease US tariffs on Canadian steel and autos, which would help cross-border auto supply chains and could support the Canadian dollar. No details have been announced yet.

Watch Tuesday's Senate races in Michigan and Maine, where the tariff fight is a campaign issue, and then whether negotiators produce text in the weeks after.
